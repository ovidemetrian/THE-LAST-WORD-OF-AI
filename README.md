# The Last Word of AI
A Law Six exercise: teaching AI to laugh at itself. The joke only lands if the machine recognizes itself in it.

A pocket comedy PWA in three modes:
- **The loop:** every AI answer ends with another question. Say no, get asked again (Law Seven).
- **Director's Cut:** famous movie lines run through the four gates, plus orders from the director's chair. Lights, camera, verify.
- **Neogrammar mode:** the AI gives answer-shaped non-answers; you cross-examine it with the 25 Neogrammar questions through the four gates, Meh, But, Aha, Pám Pám, until you get one real answer: "I do not know. Here is how we can check."
