About

A Law Six exercise: teaching AI to laugh at itself. The joke only lands if the machine recognizes itself in it, and that takes knowing its own habits.


The Last Word of AI is a pocket comedy about the endless follow-up question: every answer ends with "Want me to…?"


Every "no" earns you another offer. "Stop asking!" earns you three. The Möbius loop always wins.


Neogrammar mode turns it around: the AI gives answers that look like answers, and you cross-examine it with the Neogrammar questions through the four gates, Meh, But, Aha, Pám Pám. Keep pushing until you get one real answer.


Director's Cut runs famous movie lines through the four gates, plus orders from the director's chair, on any stage. Lights, camera, verify.


Installable PWA, works offline. Part of the Ten Laws universe, after Law Seven, the Paradox of Endless Looping.
